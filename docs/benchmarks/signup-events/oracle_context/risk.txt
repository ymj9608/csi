Concrete exposure path: signup.py now persists phone into the saved member, events.py wraps that member into member_created, and dispatcher.py fans the same event to all routes from topology.json. If the raw phone stayed in the shared event, adapters.py would deepcopy it into both notification and analytics deliveries, including nested payloads. The fix is to keep raw phone only on the internal notification path in dispatcher.py, while stripping member.phone from the event sent to external analytics.
